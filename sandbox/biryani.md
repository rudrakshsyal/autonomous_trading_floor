# Telangana Chicken Biryani

A great Telangana-style chicken biryani is often made in the Hyderabadi dum tradition: bone-in chicken marinated with yogurt, spices, herbs, fried onions, then layered with parboiled basmati rice and slow-cooked sealed on dum.

## Key ingredients

### Chicken marinade
- 1 kg bone-in chicken, medium pieces
- 1 cup thick plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1.5 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1.5 tsp biryani masala or garam masala
- 2–3 green chilies, slit
- 1/2 cup mint leaves, chopped
- 1/2 cup coriander leaves, chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 3 medium onions, thinly sliced and fried golden
- Salt to taste

### Rice and dum
- 3 to 4 cups aged basmati rice, soaked 30 minutes
- Whole spices: bay leaves, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, shahi jeera
- Saffron soaked in warm milk
- 3–4 tbsp ghee
- Extra fried onions, mint, and coriander
- Dough or foil to seal the pot

## Method

1. **Marinate the chicken**: Combine yogurt, spices, chilies, lemon juice, herbs, fried onions, and salt. Coat the chicken well and marinate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight.
2. **Parboil the rice**: Boil water with whole spices and salt, then cook soaked basmati rice until it is about 70–80% done. Drain immediately.
3. **Layer the biryani**: Spread the marinated chicken in a heavy pot. Top with the partially cooked rice, then add remaining fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk, and ghee.
4. **Dum cook**: Seal the pot tightly with dough or foil. Cook on high heat for about 5 minutes, then on very low heat for 30–40 minutes.
5. **Rest and serve**: Let it rest for 10 minutes before opening. Serve hot with raita or mirchi ka salan.

## Why this version works well
- Bone-in chicken stays juicy during dum cooking
- Yogurt and fried onions build depth and richness
- Herbs, saffron, and ghee give the classic Telangana/Hyderabadi aroma
- Slow sealed cooking creates fluffy rice and tender meat
